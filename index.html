Regulatory Insights

CBSE Governance Knowledge Hub

Key legal frameworks every school administrator and trust chairperson must maintain.

Under CBSE Affiliation Bye-Laws 2018 (Section 14) and the POCSO Act 2012, every affiliated institution MUST constitute a dedicated Child Protection Committee comprising:
  • Principal / Head of Institution (Chairman)
  • Two senior female teachers
  • One external legal or NGO member working in child welfare
  • Designated Nodal Officer for receiving and recording student disclosures
Non-constitution of this committee is a non-bailable violation leading to immediate show-cause notices and potential cancellation of CBSE affiliation.
CBSE Affiliation Bye-Laws Chapter 5 (Rule 5.3) dictates that no teacher can be employed without executing a formal Service Agreement in the exact format prescribed as 'Form 1'. This document guarantees security of tenure, clearly details EPF/Gratuity entitlements, confirms working hours, and protects the school from abrupt mid-term resignations while shielding management during surprise affiliation inspections.
CBSE mandates a strict minimum ratio of 1.5 teachers per section excluding the Principal, Physical Education Teacher, and Co-Scholastic instructors. Furthermore, under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act and CBSE circulars, every affiliated school must appoint at least one qualified Special Educator recognized by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) and a certified Wellness Counselor.
Our talent screening is a 3-tier filtration process:
  1. Normative Verification: Validating degrees (B.Ed, Master's, CTET) against NCTE and CBSE norms.
  2. Subject-Matter Live Assessment: A 20-minute conceptual demonstration evaluated by senior subject evaluators for pedagogical clarity, blackboard/smartboard work, and English fluency.
  3. Police & Document Dossier: Background check, police clearance undertaking, and Form 1 stamp signing prior to final placement.
